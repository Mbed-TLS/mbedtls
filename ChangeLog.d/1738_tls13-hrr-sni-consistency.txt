Security
  * Fix TLS 1.3 server handling of the second ClientHello after a
    HelloRetryRequest. An attacker could change SNI between the two
    ClientHellos and bypass per-host client-authentication requirements
    configured through the SNI callback. Reset state derived from the first
    ClientHello before processing the second and reject inconsistent SNI,
    cipher suite and key-share parameters. Found and reported independently
    by saikumar279 and Duong Gia Huy.
    CVE-2026-116375
