Security
   * Fix a side channel in the p256-m implementation of ECDH/ECDSA on NIST
     P-256. On some platforms, 256-bit addition and subtraction had
     data-dependent branches. A local attacker could use those to recover some
     bits of the ECDSA ephemeral scalar, possibly leading to a full private key
     recovery. This only affects configurations with
     MBEDTLS_PSA_P256M_DRIVER_ENABLED defined (either explicitly, or implicitly
     because NIST P-256 is the only supported curve and EC J-PAKE is disabled).
     Found by Zahiya Zahid (MS Information Security, MCS, NUST) and Shahzaib
     Tahir (MS Information Security, MCS, NUST). CVE-2026-93845
