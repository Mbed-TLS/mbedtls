Bugfix
   * On a TLS client, do not put a literal IPv4 or IPv6 address into the
     ClientHello server_name extension. RFC 6066 section 3 forbids address
     literals in HostName. mbedtls_ssl_set_hostname() still uses the address
     to verify the server certificate against iPAddress SANs. Reported by
     arthurtumanyan in #11099.
