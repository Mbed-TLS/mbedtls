Security
   * Fix a Spectre v1 side channel in non-authenticated ciphers that can
     leak many key bits to powerful local attackers. Reported by Oleksii
     Oleksenko (Azure Research, Microsoft) and Alvise de Faveri Tron
     (Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam). CVE-2026-93833
   * Fix possible Spectre v1 side channels in mbedtls_cipher_setkey(),
     mbedtls_cipher_crypt(), mbedtls_cipher_auth_encrypt_ext() and
     mbedtls_cipher_auth_decrypt_ext() when MBEDTLS_USE_PSA_CRYPTO is enabled.
