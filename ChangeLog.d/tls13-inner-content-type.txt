Bugfix
   * In TLS 1.3, reject a protected record whose inner content type is not
     alert, handshake or application data with
     MBEDTLS_ERR_SSL_UNEXPECTED_MESSAGE, and send a fatal unexpected_message
     alert, as required by RFC 8446. Previously, a protected
     ChangeCipherSpec record could be silently ignored, and a record with an
     unknown inner content type was rejected without the alert or, when
     MBEDTLS_SSL_DTLS_CONNECTION_ID was disabled, accepted by the record
     layer and only rejected later. Fixes #11059.
