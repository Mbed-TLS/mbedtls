Bugfix
   * When verifying a hostname, fall back to the subject CN if the
     certificate's subjectAltName extension is not critical and contains no
     DNS name, IP address or URI. Previously the CN was ignored whenever the
     extension was present, so a certificate whose subjectAltName only held,
     for example, an email address failed verification. Fixes #10095.
   * Hostname verification now fails for a certificate with an empty subject,
     unless it is an end-entity certificate with a critical subjectAltName
     extension, as required by X.509.
